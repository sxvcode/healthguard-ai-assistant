Product Name: HealthGuard Comprehensive Policy

1. Product Overview
The HealthGuard Comprehensive Policy is a health insurance policy designed to provide financial protection against hospitalization, critical illness, and selected medical expenses. The policy is intended for individuals and families seeking comprehensive health coverage.

2. Target Audience and Eligibility
- Individuals aged 18 to 65 are eligible to purchase the policy.
- Dependent children may be covered under a family plan from age 1 to 25.
- The policyholder must provide accurate health and lifestyle information during enrollment.
- Coverage is subject to underwriting approval.

3. Coverage Limits
- Base coverage: $500,000 per policy year.
- Critical Illness rider: Additional $100,000.
- The critical illness rider applies only to Cancer, Stroke, and Heart Attack.
- Coverage is valid only during the active policy period.

4. Hospitalization Benefits
All inpatient procedures are covered, subject to the strict exclusions listed in Section 7. The policy covers medically necessary hospitalization expenses, including:
- Inpatient hospitalization
- ICU charges
- Doctor consultation during hospitalization
- Nursing charges
- Diagnostic tests during hospitalization
- Emergency ambulance charges are covered up to $2,000 per policy year, EXCEPT when a non-network ambulance is used, in which case the coverage is strictly capped at $500 and the policyholder must pay a 10% co-pay
- Daycare procedures that require less than 24 hours of hospitalization

5. Pre- and Post-Hospitalization Benefits
- Pre-hospitalization expenses are covered for 30 days before admission.
- Post-hospitalization expenses are covered for 60 days after discharge.
- These expenses must be related to the approved hospitalization claim.

6. Waiting Periods
- Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start date, except for accidental injuries.
- Pre-existing conditions are subject to a 24-month waiting period.
- Specific listed conditions such as cataract, hernia, and joint replacement are covered after 12 months.
- Waiting periods apply from the policy start date or from the date of rider addition.

7. Exclusions
The policy does not cover:
- Cosmetic surgery
- Elective dental procedures
- Experimental or unproven treatments
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Non-prescribed medical expenses
- Treatment taken outside the approved hospital network unless pre-authorized
- Medical expenses arising from false declaration during enrollment
- Rhinoplasty is considered cosmetic and is excluded, UNLESS it is required due to a documented accidental injury occurring within the active policy period.

8. Premium Calculation Rules
- Base annual premium: $1,200.
- Smokers incur a 15% surcharge on the base premium.
- Critical illness rider premium: $250 annually.
- Family plan surcharge: 20% of the base premium.
- Total annual premium before tax is calculated as:
  Base Premium + Applicable Surcharges + Selected Rider Premiums.

Example:
If a smoker selects the critical illness rider:
Base premium = $1,200
Smoker surcharge = $180
Critical illness rider = $250
Total annual premium before tax = $1,630

9. Claim Process
For cashless claims:
- The policyholder must visit a network hospital.
- The policyholder must present the policy ID and identity proof.
- Planned hospitalization must be notified at least 72 hours in advance.
- Emergency hospitalization must be reported within 48 hours.

For reimbursement claims:
- The policyholder must pay the hospital bill first.
- The claim form and supporting documents must be submitted within 30 days of discharge.

10. Required Documents for Claims
The following documents may be required:
- Completed claim form
- Hospital discharge summary
- Final hospital bill
- Doctor prescriptions
- Diagnostic reports
- Pharmacy bills
- Policy ID
- Government-issued identity proof

11. Renewal and Cancellation
- The policy is renewable annually.
- Renewal must be completed before the policy expiry date.
- A grace period of 15 days is allowed for premium payment.
- Coverage is not active during unpaid grace period claims unless renewal payment is completed.
- The insurer may cancel the policy in case of fraud or material misrepresentation.

12. Scope Limitation
This policy document provides information only about the HealthGuard Comprehensive Policy. It does not provide medical diagnosis, legal advice, tax advice, or investment advice.

13. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are pre-existing diseases covered?
A: Yes. Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 24-month waiting period.

Q: Is cosmetic surgery covered?
A: No. Cosmetic surgery is excluded.

Q: Are elective dental procedures covered?
A: No. Elective dental procedures are excluded.

Q: What is the critical illness rider?
A: The critical illness rider provides an additional $100,000 benefit for Cancer, Stroke, and Heart Attack.

Q: Do smokers pay more?
A: Yes. Smokers incur a 15% surcharge on the base annual premium.

Q: What is the base annual premium?
A: The base annual premium is $1,200.

Q: What is the maximum base coverage?
A: The base coverage limit is $500,000 per policy year.
